#  My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## Overview
**Stranger Things** is an American science fiction horror drama series created by the Duffer Brothers. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, it follows a group of friends investigating the disappearance of a boy and the appearance of a mysterious girl with telekinetic powers.


---

###  Main Characters
1. **Eleven / El** — A girl with telepathic and telekinetic abilities
2. **Mike Wheeler** — The leader of the group and Eleven's closest friend
3. **Will Byers** — The boy who goes missing
4. **Dustin Henderson** — Smart and funny, with great hair
5. **Lucas Sinclair** — Practical and brave



###  Why I Enjoy Watching It
- Great blend of *80s nostalgia* and modern storytelling
- The friendships feel very **real and heartfelt**
- Every season keeps you guessing until the very end
- Amazing soundtrack that sets the mood perfectly



###  Seasons
- **Season 1:** The disappearance of Will Byers
- **Season 2:** Strange things return to Hawkins
- **Season 3:** Summer of 1985 — new threats emerge
- **Season 4:** The darkest season yet — new dangers in a different place
